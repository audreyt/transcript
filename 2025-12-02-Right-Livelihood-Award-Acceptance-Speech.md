# 2025-12-02 Right Livelihood Award Acceptance Speech

### Audrey Tang:

Four million years. Plate against plate.<br>
Taiwan: forever rising, forever starward.

Freedom and democracy did not fall from the sky.<br>
They were forged under intense pressure —<br>
cultivated by the blood, sweat and tears of those who came before us.

Polarization has long been considered a fire to be extinguished.<br>
But in Taiwan, we learned a new way.

We learned that as the youngest tectonic island in the world,<br>
crashing plates<br>
give rise to a majestic verdancy.

We built a geothermal democracy —<br>
the crashing plates turned<br>
into positive energy for renewal.

This is the challenge for all democracies facing backsliding:<br>
not to silence<br>
but to channel conflict.

AI must not divide us with outrage.<br>
It must connect us through uncommon ground.

We the people are the true superintelligence.

To be Taiwan’s first Right Livelihood Laureate<br>
is not my honor alone — it is ours.

To everyone who etched their mark on digital freedom,<br>
to everyone waging peace across the globe:<br>
Thank you.

婆娑之洋，美麗之島。<br>
公民之國，萬華之中。

Swirling ocean. Beautiful islands.<br>
A transcultural republic of citizens.

Let’s free the future — together.
